# ScreenShip Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-27

## Summary

ScreenShip is a local-first screenshot capture and editing extension for Chrome.

We do not track you, profile you, sell your data, or transmit your screenshot data back to us.
ScreenShip does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, remote logging, or a required account system.

## What ScreenShip Handles

To perform the features you ask it to perform, ScreenShip may locally handle:

- Website content included in a screenshot
- The URL and page title of the page being captured
- Capture metadata such as capture time, viewport size, and page height
- User-created edits such as drawings, text, blur regions, shapes, and layer settings

This data is handled solely so the extension can capture, edit, copy, and export screenshots for you.

## What We Collect

We do not collect personal data from you.

We do not receive your screenshots, page content, URLs, annotations, or exported images.

We do not operate a backend service that receives screenshot or editing data from ScreenShip.

## How Data Is Used

ScreenShip uses data locally on your device to provide its core features:

- Capture a selected area, the visible viewport, or a scrolling full page
- Open the built-in editor
- Let you annotate and refine the screenshot with layers and editing tools
- Copy or export the final image
- Embed capture metadata in exported files when the chosen format supports it

## Local Storage

ScreenShip may temporarily store capture session data in your browser so it can hand a freshly captured image from the capture flow to the editor.

ScreenShip may also store small local preferences, such as theme choice, in your browser.

This information is stored locally for the extension to function. It is not transmitted to us.

## Sharing and Data Transfer

We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer user data to us or to third parties for advertising, analytics, profiling, or monetization.

ScreenShip does not transmit screenshot content, browsing data, or user edits to our servers because there are no servers involved in the core capture, edit, and export workflow.

## Exported Files

When you export an image, the file is created for you locally.

Depending on the export format, ScreenShip may embed metadata such as:

- Source URL
- Page title
- Capture timestamp
- Capture mode

This metadata is part of the exported file itself and remains under your control.

## Permissions

ScreenShip requests browser permissions only to provide screenshot and editing features, such as:

- Access to the active tab so the current page can be captured when you ask for it
- Script injection on user-requested pages so selection and full-page capture can work
- Temporary local storage so captured sessions can be passed into the editor
- Clipboard access when you choose to copy an image

## Changes

If ScreenShip's data practices change, this policy should be updated to reflect the new behavior before release.
